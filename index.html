How It Works

1

Set Your Name

Open app settings and set your name so ChatViewApp can recognize your messages in chat exports.

2

Export from WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp, select the chat you want to save, go to chat settings, and export the chat. Choose whether to include media or not.

3

Import to ChatViewApp

Upload the exported chat file to ChatViewApp. Our parser will automatically extract all messages, images, and documents.

4

View and Manage

Rename your chat for easy identification, browse through messages, view images, and export any documents you need.
